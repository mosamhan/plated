Why it’s different

Welcome to Plated

Great restaurants are built one great dish at a time.

01
Rate

Every plate gets its own score.

Rate each dish from 0 to 10. A restaurant’s Plated’s Rating is built from every dish rated there, so the best restaurants rise to the top because of the food they serve. Score 9.0 or higher and a dish earns a flame.

02
Watch

Platos: dish stories in motion.

Watch short videos about one dish at a time, or browse a grid of what’s trending, top rated, and most reordered near you. It’s where the next big thing in food shows up first.

03
Discover

Find exactly what you’re craving.

Filter by cafes, bakeries, bars, pizza, and more. Switch your location to browse a new city, or search for a specific dish or place. Deals from local spots show up right here too.

04
Rank

See what’s actually the best.

Switch to Ranks to see the top rated restaurants and plates, nearby or across the world. No paid placements. Just what people are rating highest.

05
Map

Every great spot, right around you.

See top rated restaurants, cafes, bars, and bakeries on a live map. Filter by what you’re craving, check each spot’s Plated’s Rating, and get directions in one tap.

06
Reorder

The highest praise in food.

A reorder is the best compliment a dish can get. Plated tracks how many people ordered a plate again, then hands you off to DoorDash, Uber Eats, or pickup so you can order it too.

Creators earn, transparently.

Food creators earn when their videos lead to a real order. That has nothing to do with how they rate a dish, and it’s always disclosed. No one can pay for placement, and no one can buy a rating.
