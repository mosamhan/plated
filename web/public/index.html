Welcome to Plated

Most food apps rate restaurants. Plated rates the dish.

Rate

Every plate gets its own score.

Rate the individual dish, 0–10 — not a vibe score for the whole restaurant. A restaurant’s Plated’s Rating is the cumulative average of every plate rated there, and anything scoring 9.0+ earns a flame.

Discover

Platos: dish stories in motion.

Swipe a TikTok-style feed of short creator clips about one specific dish, or browse a grid of what’s trending, top-rated, and most reordered near you.

Reorder

The highest praise in food.

A reorder is the strongest trust signal a dish can earn. Plated tracks how many people ordered a plate again, then hands you off to DoorDash, Uber Eats, or pickup to order that exact dish.

Creators earn, transparently.

Food creators earn on orders attributed to their plates — decoupled from how they rate a dish, and disclosed on every surface that shows monetized content. Never pay for placement, never buy a rating.
